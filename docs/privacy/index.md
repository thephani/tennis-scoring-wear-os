# Privacy Policy for Tennis Score for Wear OS

Effective date: September 26, 2026

Tennis Score for Wear OS is an offline singles tennis scoring app. It does not require an account and does not contain advertising, analytics, or an internet connection.

## Information stored on your watch

The app saves your point entries, match scores, match format, first server, settings, and completed match results on your watch so that you can resume a match and review results. Completed results include the match date, winner, and set scores. This information stays in the app's local storage on your watch. The app does not send it to the developer or share it with third parties. App data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer.

## Data collection and sharing

The app does not collect personal data from your watch, sell data, or share app data with third parties. It requests no network permission.

## Your choices

You can remove saved match data by clearing the app's storage in your watch settings or uninstalling the app. Clearing storage removes your match history and settings as well.
