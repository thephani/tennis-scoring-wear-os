# Tennis Score for Wear OS - Play listing draft

App name: Tennis Score for Wear OS

Short description: Score singles tennis matches on your Wear OS watch, even offline.

Full description:

Keep score on court with a tennis scorer made for your watch. Tap YOU or OPP for each point and see games, sets, tiebreaks, and the current server at a glance.

- Choose best of three or five sets
- Play a full deciding set or a 10-point match tiebreak
- Undo a point and resume an unfinished match
- Review completed match results

Tennis Score works on a Wear OS watch without a phone, account, or internet connection. Designed for singles matches.
